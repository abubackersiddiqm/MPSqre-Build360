# MPSqre Build360 v0.20.0

Phase 20 introduces governed People Operations and payroll evidence for Build360.

## Scope

- Tenant-scoped organization departments and reporting hierarchy
- Employment contracts with independent approval evidence
- Versioned leave policies and annual balances
- Self-service leave requests with overlap and balance controls
- Weekly timesheets with project allocation and maker-checker approval
- Payroll runs and employee entries with fixed-precision values
- Payroll lock, approval, posting, cancellation, and SHA-256 evidence
- Twenty-four semantic permissions, audit records, outbox events, and a responsive People Operations workspace

## Important boundary

The payroll foundation intentionally excludes jurisdiction-specific statutory computation until regional policies are configured, legally reviewed, and independently validated. Phase 20 produces governed payroll evidence; it is not a certified statutory payroll engine.
